Pricing FAQ

Good to know

Are these prices fixed for every vehicle?

No. Prices are fixed per vehicle category (Hatchback, Sedan, SUV or Full Size SUV), so two cars in the same category share the same listed price.

How do I know which vehicle category my car belongs to?

Go by body size and type. Small cars such as an Alto or Picanto are Hatchbacks, cars such as a Civic or City are Sedans, and larger vehicles fall under SUV or Full Size SUV. Not sure? Call or WhatsApp us and we'll tell you.

Can the final price change after inspection?

It can. If extra parts, repairs or vehicle-specific requirements are found, we'll tell you before going ahead. Denting work is always priced after inspection.

Do service prices include parts?

Items marked as labour cover workmanship only. Parts are not part of the listed service price, and we confirm any parts needed for your vehicle before starting.

Can I book a service directly from this page?

Yes. Use the Book a Service button to reach our contact page, or call or WhatsApp us to confirm availability and a time that suits you.

Do you provide vehicle-specific filter options?

Yes. Use the Oil & Filter Pricing section above to choose your make and vehicle and see the oil, air and AC filter prices that apply.
